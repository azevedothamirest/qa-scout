Who can use it

Anyone, with no sign-up and no cost. There is no account, paid plan or usage limit.

Your data

Sessions, notes and screenshots stay only in your browser's local storage. QA Scout has no server and receives none of your data. Details in the privacy policy.

Responsible use

  • Use QA Scout only on systems you are authorized to test.
  • Prefer test environments with fake data: screenshots show what is on screen.
  • Review notes and screenshots before sharing a report.
  • Follow your company's rules on tools and data. Material for your security team is in docs/seguranca.md (in Portuguese).

Claude mode (MCP, optional)

The MCP server runs on your computer and only works if you install it. In this mode, the text of visited pages is sent to Claude, by Anthropic, under Anthropic's terms. The Chrome Web Store extension does not use this mode.

Security

Found a vulnerability? Report it privately to contato.qascout@gmail.com or through GitHub. You will get a first reply within 7 days.

Changes
